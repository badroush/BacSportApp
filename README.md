## 📌 Overview

**BacSportApp** is an application designed to support the organization and management of activities and data related to the **Physical Education examination of the Tunisian Baccalaureate (Bac Sport)**.

The project aims to provide a digital solution for organizing information, simplifying management tasks and facilitating access to relevant data.

It is an example of a **business-oriented application developed around a concrete administrative and educational need**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of BacSportApp are to:

- 📋 Organize Bac Sport information
- 🏃 Manage physical education activities
- 👨‍🎓 Facilitate student-related data management
- 📊 Structure examination-related information
- 📱 Provide a practical digital interface
- ⚙️ Reduce repetitive administrative operations
- 🗂️ Improve organization and accessibility of data

---

## ✨ Main Features

Depending on the configured modules, the application provides functionality around:

- Student information management
- Bac Sport activity management
- Data organization
- Examination-related information
- Search and consultation
- Structured records
- Digital management of operational information
